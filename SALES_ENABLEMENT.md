## 🎙️ 3. 5–7 Minute Live Demo Script Outline

- **Minute 0–1: Problem Alignment**: Confirm prospect's team size, current tracking method (spreadsheets/biometrics), and main friction points.
- **Minute 1–3: Employee Experience**: Show 3-second 1-click web clock-in, project selection tag, and transparent employee PTO balance view.
- **Minute 3–5: Manager & Ops View**: Showcase the Live Daily Roster, switch department filters, and highlight the automated Late & Absent Exception Log with grace periods.
- **Minute 5–6: 1-Click Payroll Export**: Trigger CSV export formatted for Gusto/Rippling in real time.
- **Minute 6–7: Q&A & Next Steps**: Address custom shift or security questions, schedule 14-day trial onboarding session.
