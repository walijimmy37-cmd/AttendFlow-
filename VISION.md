# AttendFlow - Long-Term Vision & Ambition Level Framework

This document defines the 10-year north-star vision, target ambition scale, and strategic trade-off framework for AttendFlow.

---

## 🌌 1. North-Star Vision (10-Year Horizon)

> **"To become the global intelligent operating system for workforce operations, enabling 1 million growing businesses to eliminate administrative waste, protect team trust, and operate with real-time operational clarity."**

AttendFlow envisions a future where employee attendance, shift scheduling, leave management, and payroll reconciliation are completely frictionless—moving from manual timestamp tracking to automated, trust-preserving, background-verified workforce synchronization.

---

## 🎯 2. Ambition Level & Scale Models

AttendFlow evaluates 3 distinct long-term corporate growth pathways:

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Model A: Bootstrapped | Model B: High-Growth  | Model C: Venture-Scale|
| Capital-Efficient SaaS| Regional Leader       | Global Category Player|
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| - Target: $3M–$10M ARR| - Target: $10M–$30M   | - Target: $50M+ ARR   |
| - Team: 10–25 headcount| - Team: 30–80 headcount| - Team: 150+ headcount|
| - Funding: Self-funded| - Funding: Seed / RBF | - Funding: Institutional|
| - Focus: Profitability| - Focus: Market Depth | - Focus: Hyper-Growth |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
```

### Strategic Focus Choice (Model B -> Model C Bridge)
AttendFlow prioritizes **Model A/B (Capital-Efficient Bootstrapping with High-Growth Option)** during Stages 1 & 2. We build a profitable cash-flow positive foundation first, preserving full optionality to accelerate into VC-backed scaling once unit economics (LTV:CAC > 8x) and repeatable acquisition channels are validated.
