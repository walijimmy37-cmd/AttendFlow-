# AttendFlow - Capital Strategy & Financial Model

This document outlines funding philosophies, investment triggers, use-of-funds breakdowns, and financial guardrails for AttendFlow.

---

## 💰 1. Funding Philosophy & Capital Strategy

AttendFlow follows a **Capital-Efficient Growth Strategy**:
1. **Stage 1 (0 to $5k MRR)**: 100% Bootstrapped / Founder-Funded. Low burn, high-efficiency execution.
2. **Stage 2 ($5k to $25k MRR)**: Non-dilutive Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) or Angel Round to fund sales expansion without early cap table dilution.
3. **Stage 3 ($25k+ MRR)**: Strategic Institutional Seed Round ($1.5M - $3.0M) *only if* unit economics dictate a 5x return on accelerated paid acquisition and channel expansion.

---

## 🚦 2. Capital Raise Triggers

We only raise external equity capital when all 4 triggers are satisfied:

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               CAPITAL RAISE TRIGGERS                            |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------+
| Trigger Requirement                                | Benchmark Metric           |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------+
| 1. Proven Product-Market Fit                       | Net Revenue Retention ≥110%|
| 2. Validated Unit Economics                        | LTV : CAC ≥ 6.0x           |
| 3. Rapid Payback Period                            | CAC Payback < 5 Months     |
| 4. Predictable Acquisition Engine                  | >30 Inbound Leads / Week   |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------+
```

## 📊 3. Use of Funds Allocation Model ($1.5M Seed Scenario)

```text
[ $1.5M Seed Capital Allocations ]
├── 45% ($675k) -> Sales & Growth (2 SDRs, Marketing Manager, Ad Spend)
├── 35% ($525k) -> Engineering & Product (2 Full-Stack Devs, Security Compliance)
└── 20% ($300k) -> Operations, G&A, and 18-Month Cash Reserve
```

---

## 🛡️ 4. Financial Guardrails & 24-Month Pro Forma Runway

- **Min Cash Reserve**: Maintain a strict minimum of **18 months operating runway**.
- **Max Burn Ceiling**: Monthly Net Burn must not exceed **$15,000** until MRR surpasses **$20,000**.
- **Gross Profit Margin Target**: Maintain software gross profit margins of **≥ 85%**.

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                          24-MONTH HIGH-LEVEL PRO FORMA                          |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+-----------------+
| Timeline              | Monthly Revenue   | Monthly Burn      | Net Cash Flow   |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+-----------------+
| Months 1–6 (Launch)   | $2,500            | $6,000            | -$3,500         |
| Months 7–12 (Growth)  | $15,000           | $12,000           | +$3,000         |
| Months 13–18 (Scale)  | $45,000           | $28,000           | +$17,000        |
```
