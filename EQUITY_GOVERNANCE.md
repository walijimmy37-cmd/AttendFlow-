# AttendFlow - Equity Structure, Incentives & Corporate Governance

This document outlines the equity cap table structure, employee option pool, performance incentive plans, and corporate governance rules for AttendFlow.

---

## 🍰 1. Cap Table & Equity Structure (Stage 1 & 2)

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               CAP TABLE DISTRIBUTION                            |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Stakeholder                        | Equity Share (%)      | Vesting Terms      |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+

+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
```

---

## 🏆 2. Employee Incentive & Compensation Design

To attract top talent in a capital-efficient environment, AttendFlow pairs competitive base salaries with performance-driven incentives:

- **Growth Compensation (Sales/SDR)**: Base salary + **10% commission** on Year-1 Contract Value for all closed-won accounts.
- **Product & CS Bonus**: Quarterly cash bonus tied to achieving target **Net Revenue Retention (NRR ≥ 110%)** and zero high-severity downtime events.
- **Milestone Option Grants**: Early employees receive equity grants that vest upon hitting company MRR thresholds ($10k, $25k, $50k MRR).

## 🏛️ 3. Corporate Governance & Decision Matrix

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            DECISION RIGHTS MATRIX                               |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------------------+
| Decision Category     | Authority Level       | Required Approvals              |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------------------+
| Vision & GTM Strategy | Founders Only         | Unanimous Founder Approval      |
| Pricing & Packaging   | Founders & Sales Lead | Founder Sign-off                |
| Cap Table & Funding   | Board of Directors    | Majority Board Vote             |
| Product Sprint Backlog| Product / Engineering | CTO Sign-off                    |

```

### Investor Communication Cadence
- **Monthly Update Email**: Sent to advisors and investors on the 5th of each month (KPIs, Cash Balance, Wins, Challenges, Asks).
- **Quarterly Board Meeting**: 60-minute formal board review of financial pro forma, audit results, and strategic initiatives.
