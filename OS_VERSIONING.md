# AttendFlow - OS Change Management & Versioning System

This document specifies the versioning policy, change proposal workflows, and document sunset rules for the AttendFlow OS documentation suite.

---

## 🏷️ 1. Versioning Protocol

Major strategic documents (`VISION.md`, `GTM.md`, `REVENUE.md`, `CAPITAL.md`, `ATTENDFLOW_OS.md`) maintain semantic version numbers:

- **Major Version (v1.0 -> v2.0)**: Substantial structural shifts (e.g., changing primary ICP, pivoting pricing tiers, raising Seed capital).
- **Minor Version (v1.0 -> v1.1)**: Incremental updates (e.g., adding a new lead magnet, tweaking weekly meeting agendas, updating KPI targets).

---

## 📝 2. Change Proposal Process

To propose a modification to an AttendFlow OS specification document:

1. **Submit Proposal**: Draft a 1-page proposal outlining: Context, Proposed Change, Expected Impact, and Potential Risks.
2. **Review Window**: Review during the Friday Retrospective or Monthly Checkpoint (`PLANNING_CALENDAR.md`).
3. **Approval**: Update document version number and record entry in the Central Decision Log (`README_GOVERNANCE.md`).

---

## 🌅 3. Document Sunset & Archive Policy

- **Archiving**: Outdated specifications or completed sprint plans are moved to `/archive/` with a header indicating sunset date and link to the replacement file.
- **Bi-Annual Audit**: Every 6 months, the Operations Lead reviews all root markdown files to prune obsolete references.
