# AttendFlow - Unified Planning & Review Calendar

This document defines the recurring annual, quarterly, monthly, and weekly calendar rhythms, meeting agendas, and planning templates.

---

## 📅 1. Master Planning Calendar Rhythms

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Cadence               | Schedule              | Primary Purpose       |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Annual Strategy Review| Q4 (November)         | Set 12-mo vision, budget|
| Quarterly OKR Planning| 1st Week of Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4| 90-day targets & bets |
| Monthly Checkpoint    | Last Business Day / Mo| P&L, runway, hiring audit|
| Weekly Alignment      | Mondays @ 09:00 AM    | Metric review & weekly bets|
| Friday Retrospective  | Fridays @ 04:30 PM    | Retro & ICE experiment log|
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
```

---

## 📝 2. Meeting Agenda & Document Templates

### A. Monthly Checkpoint Agenda (60 Mins)
1. **Financial Health & P&L (15 mins)**: Review MRR, Gross Margin, Net Burn, and Cash Runway vs `CAPITAL.md`.
2. **Channel Performance (15 mins)**: CAC, LTV, and conversion rate audit across Inbound & Outbound.
3. **Product & Roadmap Sync (15 mins)**: Review feature delivery against `ROADMAP.md` and user feedback in `CUSTOMER_LEARNING.md`.
4. **Talent & Hiring Audit (15 mins)**: Evaluate hiring triggers against `TEAM_ORG.md` and `FOUNDER_PLAYBOOK.md`.
