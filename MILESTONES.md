# AttendFlow - Growth Milestones & Stage Gates

This document defines the 3 growth stages, entry/exit criteria, milestone metrics, and strategic focus areas for scaling AttendFlow.

---

## 🚦 1. Stage Gate Framework Overview

```text
[ Stage 1: Validation ] ---> [ Stage 2: Early Traction ] ---> [ Stage 3: Scale Prep ]
(0 to 10 Paid Accounts)      (10 to 50 Paid Accounts)        (50 to 200+ Accounts)
```

---

## 🏁 2. Detailed Stage Specifications

### Stage 1: Validation (0 to 10 Paying Accounts)
- **Primary Goal**: Prove core value proposition and willingness to pay.
- **Entry Criteria**: Product launched with 1-click check-in, live roster, and CSV export.
- **Exit Criteria**: 10 Paying Customers ($1,000+ MRR) with < 3% monthly churn.
- **Key Focus**: Founder-led outbound sales, manual demo onboarding, and rapid user feedback synthesis.

### Stage 2: Early Traction (10 to 50 Paying Accounts)
- **Primary Goal**: Establish 1 to 2 repeatable customer acquisition channels.
- **Entry Criteria**: 10 Paid Accounts, 2 published customer case studies.
- **Exit Criteria**: 50 Paying Customers ($6,000+ MRR), CAC Payback < 5 months.
- **Key Focus**: Inbound SEO content engine, automated trial onboarding, and partner program launch.

### Stage 3: Scale Prep (50 to 200+ Paying Accounts)
- **Primary Goal**: Build operational infrastructure for repeatable, scalable growth.
- **Entry Criteria**: 50 Paid Accounts, $6,000+ MRR, 5 active partner referral channels.
- **Exit Criteria**: 200+ Paying Customers ($25,000+ MRR), SOC2 Type II certification.
- **Key Focus**: Hiring first sales/support team members, expanding HRIS API integrations, and multi-page site migration.
