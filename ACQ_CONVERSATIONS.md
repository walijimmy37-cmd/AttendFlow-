# AttendFlow - Inflection Point Framework: Acquisition & M&A Conversations

This document outlines evaluation criteria, process steps, data room protocols, and founder alignment filters for inbound M&A approaches.

---

## 🚪 1. Inbound Acquisition Evaluation Filters

When approached by potential acquirers (HRIS vendors, Payroll platforms, private equity), evaluate against 4 strategic filters:

1. **Strategic Fit**: Does the acquirer share AttendFlow's commitment to frictionless workforce administration?
2. **Valuation Multiple**: Is the proposed valuation ≥ 4.0x - 8.0x ARR (or 10.0x+ ARR for category-leading NRR)?
3. **Team Continuity**: Are key engineers and team members provided meaningful retention incentives and clear roles?
4. **Founder Personal Alignment**: Does the transaction satisfy liquidity goals defined in `EXIT_OPTIONS.md`?

## 🔐 3. Data Room Checklist for M&A

- **Financials**: 3-Year P&L, Monthly MRR schedule, Cap Table (`CAPITAL.md`, `EQUITY_GOVERNANCE.md`).
- **Product & IP**: System Architecture Map, Codebase audit logs, Security Certifications (`LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`).
- **Customer Contracts**: Standard ToS, DPA, Top 10 customer contracts.
- **Operations & Systems**: AttendFlow OS Documentation Suite (`ATTENDFLOW_OS.md`).
