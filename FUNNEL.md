# AttendFlow - Conversion Funnel & Mathematical Model

This document outlines the 4-stage customer acquisition funnel, conversion metrics, stage ownership, and mathematical revenue projection framework for AttendFlow.

---

## 📊 1. Funnel Architecture & Ownership

```text
[ Stage 1: Visitor ] ---> (3.0% Conv) ---> [ Stage 2: Qualified Lead ]
                                                    |
                                               (40.0% Conv)
                                                    v
[ Stage 4: Paid Customer ] <--- (25.0% Conv) <--- [ Stage 3: Demo / Trial ]
```

| Stage | Definition | Primary Action | Owner | Target Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Visitor** | Anonymous web traffic on `attendflow.io` | Engages with interactive preview or pricing table | Marketing | Total monthly unique visitors |
| **2. Qualified Lead** | Prospect who completed demo modal or survey | Submits work email, team size, and primary use case | Growth Marketing | Visitor-to-Lead Rate (≥ 3.0%) |
| **3. Demo / Trial** | Lead who launched a 14-day trial or held a sales call | Conducts 1-click check-ins and tests CSV payroll exports | Sales / RevOps | Lead-to-Trial/Demo Rate (≥ 40.0%) |
| **4. Paid Customer** | Prospect converted to monthly/annual plan | Upgrades account via credit card / invoicing | Customer Success | Trial-to-Paid Rate (≥ 25.0%) |

---

## 🔢 2. Mathematical Conversion Model (Monthly Baseline)

### Standard Target Scenario (5,000 Visitors / Month)

- **Monthly Visitors**: 5,000
- **Lead Conversion Rate (3.0%)**: 150 Qualified Leads
- **Demo / Trial Rate (40.0%)**: 60 Active Demos / Trials
- **Paid Conversion Rate (25.0%)**: 15 New Paid Customers
- **Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA)**: $120 / month (Growth plan base + seat add-ons)
- **New Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: $1,800 / month added per 5k visitors

---

## 🔁 3. Account Expansion & Retention Mechanics

1. **Seat Accrual**: As client teams scale from 15 to 40 employees, monthly revenue automatically expands ($3/user/mo).
2. **Module Add-Ons**: Upgrading from Starter to Growth ($49/mo) or Enterprise ($99/mo) to unlock geofenced check-in and custom audit logs.
3. **Annual Plan Discount**: 20% discount offered on upfront annual commitments to maximize net revenue retention (NRR ≥ 110%).
