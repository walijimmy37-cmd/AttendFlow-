# AttendFlow - Strategy-to-Execution Operating Loop

This document specifies the closed-loop feedback system connecting long-term vision with quarterly planning, weekly execution, and continuous retrospective learning.

---

## 🔁 1. The Strategy-to-Execution Cycle

```text
       +-------------------------------------------------------+
       |             1. 12-MONTH STRATEGY                      |
       |  (Vision, Milestone Stage, Target ARR - `VISION.md`)  |
       +---------------------------+---------------------------+
                                   |
                                   v
       +-------------------------------------------------------+
       |             2. QUARTERLY OKRs & SPRINTS               |
       |    (3-5 Core Objectives & KRs - `90_DAY_PLAN.md`)     |
       +---------------------------+---------------------------+
                                   |
                                   v
       +-------------------------------------------------------+
       |             3. WEEKLY EXECUTION & BETS                |
       |  (Time-blocking & ICE Experiments - `OPERATING_RHYTHM`) |
       +---------------------------+---------------------------+
                                   |
                                   v
       +-------------------------------------------------------+
       |             4. RETROSPECTIVE & FEEDBACK               |
       |  (Friday Data Review & Feedback - `CUSTOMER_LEARNING`)|
       +---------------------------+---------------------------+
                                   |
                                   +---> Loop Back to Adjust Plans
```

---

## 🎯 2. Annual Strategic Themes Framework

Each fiscal year, leadership establishes 3 to 4 non-negotiable strategic themes:

- **Theme 1 (GTM Acceleration)**: Validate repeatable outbound sales in software consultancies & BPOs.
- **Theme 2 (Product & Integrations)**: Deliver zero-friction 1-click payroll CSV exports for Gusto and Rippling.
- **Theme 3 (Capital Efficiency)**: Achieve CAC Payback < 5 months with a minimum 85% gross margin.

---

## 📊 3. Translating Strategy into Quarterly OKRs

Every 90 days, strategic themes translate into Objective & Key Result (OKR) targets:

### Example Quarterly OKR Set (Stage 1 -> Stage 2 Transition)
- **Objective**: Establish predictable inbound and outbound pipeline in ICP 1 (Tech Agencies).
  - **KR 1**: Generate 150 qualified demo requests.
  - **KR 2**: Close 15 new paid growth accounts ($2,550+ MRR).
  - **KR 3**: Maintain trial activation rate ≥ 65%.
  - **KR 4**: Achieve logo churn < 2.0% per month.

---

## 🔄 4. The Feedback & Adjustment Mechanism

- **Weekly Adjustments**: Tactical changes (A/B copy tweaks, outbound subject lines) executed based on Friday retro metrics (`DASHBOARDS.md`).
- **Monthly Adjustments**: Re-allocate ad spend or SDR focus if acquisition channels underperform defined CAC benchmarks.
- **Quarterly Adjustments**: Re-evaluate product roadmap and hiring plans based on milestone stage gate achievements (`MILESTONES.md`).
