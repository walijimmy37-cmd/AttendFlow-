# AttendFlow - OS Feedback System & Quarterly OS Reviews

This document defines the feedback collection channels, usage signal indicators, and quarterly review protocol for maintaining the AttendFlow OS.

---

## 👂 1. Feedback Channels for the OS

Team members and contractors submit feedback on AttendFlow OS specifications via 3 dedicated channels:

1. **Slack/Teams Channel (`#attendflow-os-feedback`)**: Instant channel for reporting confusing text, missing templates, or outdated metrics.
2. **Monthly OS Feedback Survey**: Anonymous 3-question pulse survey on documentation clarity, speed of decision-making, and process friction.
3. **Friday Retro Open Floor**: Dedicated 5-minute item at the end of Friday retrospectives to capture process improvement suggestions.

---

## 📊 2. Usage Signals & Diagnostic Indicators

Leadership tracks 3 diagnostic signals to assess documentation health:

- **Reference Frequency**: How often OS files are linked or cited in meeting notes, PRs, and sales tickets.
- **Onboarding Friction**: Specific steps in `ONBOARDING.md` where new hires require extra clarification.
- **Decision Drift**: Instances where team members make strategic or pricing choices without referencing `DECISION_FRAMEWORKS.md`.

---

## 🗓️ 3. Quarterly OS Review Protocol

During the final week of each quarter, leadership conducts a 60-minute **OS Maintenance & Pruning Session**:

- [ ] Review all entries in the `#attendflow-os-feedback` log.
- [ ] Prune or rewrite documents tagged for clarity improvements.
- [ ] Sunset obsolete process docs by moving them to `/archive/` per `OS_VERSIONING.md`.
