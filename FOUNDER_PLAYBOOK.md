## 📋 2. Operator Checklist Cadence

### Daily Checklist (Morning & Afternoon)
- [ ] Review inbound demo requests & dispatch response within **< 15 minutes**.
- [ ] Check active trial user check-in activity and offer setup assistance.
- [ ] Execute 20 LinkedIn outbound touchpoints.

### Weekly Checklist (Fridays)
- [ ] Conduct 30-minute Leadership Metric Review.
- [ ] Synthesize customer feedback into `CUSTOMER_LEARNING.md`.
- [ ] Publish 1 flagship blog post and 2 social carousels.

### Monthly Checklist (End of Month)
- [ ] Review P&L, MRR growth, and cash runway in `REVENUE.md`.
- [ ] Audit acquisition channels and adjust spend allocation.
- [ ] Conduct 1-on-1 monthly alignment with key channel partners.
