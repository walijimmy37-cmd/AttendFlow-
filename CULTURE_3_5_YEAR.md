# AttendFlow - Culture & Team Vision at Scale (2026–2031)

This document specifies how core values scale as the company grows from a founder-led team to a 40+ person organization.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Scaling Core Values Without Dilution

As AttendFlow grows from 3 to 45 team members, our core principles from `CULTURE.md` translate into organizational behaviors:

```text
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            SCALED CULTURE BEHAVIORS                              |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Core Value            | How It Operates at Scale (40+ Person Team)               |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Obsess Over        | • Internal processes must be friction-free (< 3 clicks). |
|    Time Saved         | • Meetings over 3 people require a written agenda and    |
|                       |   a pre-circulated memo (`OPERATING_RHYTHM.md`).         |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Evidence Over      | • Product proposals require user telemetry or A/B test   |
|    Opinion            |   data, not senior title authority.                      |
|                       | • All PRs cite specific user feedback or metric impact.  |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Written-First      | • Default to asynchronous Slack updates and clear OS     |
|    Autonomy           |   markdown documentation over synchronous syncs.         |
|                       | • Every decision references `DECISION_FRAMEWORKS.md`.    |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 👥 2. Team Shape, Density & Remote-First Operations

- **High Talent Density over Headcount**: We prioritize hiring fewer, highly skilled, self-directed builders compensated at top-of-market rates over bloated mid-level management tiers.
- **Geographic Structure**: Remote-first organization operating across 3 core time zone hubs:
  1. **Americas Hub**: Executive leadership, Enterprise Sales, Customer Success.
  2. **Europe / UK Hub**: Product Design, Growth Marketing, Legal/Compliance.
  3. **APAC Hub**: Core Full-Stack Engineering, Infrastructure, QA Automation.

---

## 👑 3. Leadership Philosophy: High Context, High Autonomy

- **Direct & Kind Feedback**: We conduct radical candor reviews using clear metrics (`METRICS_INDEX.md`) and role playbooks (`PLAYBOOK_*`).
- **Context over Control**: Leaders provide clear strategic boundaries, metric targets, and budget guardrails, then grant complete execution autonomy to domain owners.
