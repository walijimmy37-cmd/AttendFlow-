# AttendFlow - Year 1 Weekly Execution Framework

This document details the weekly execution priorities, planning templates, big bet experiment tags, and review prompts for Year 1.

---

## 🗓️ 1. Weekly Execution Rhythm & Time Allocation

In accordance with `OPERATING_RHYTHM.md`, the team executes on a strict weekly cycle:

- **Monday Morning (9:00 AM - 10:00 AM)**: Weekly Alignment & Priority Lock.
- **Tuesday - Thursday**: Uninterrupted Deep Work Execution (No internal syncs).
- **Friday Afternoon (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM)**: Retrospective, Metric Review & Retrospective Log.

---

## 📝 2. Weekly Planning Template

Every Monday, the team documents 3–5 high-leverage weekly priorities tied directly to quarterly OKRs (`YEAR_1_QUARTERLY.md`):

```markdown
### 📌 Weekly Priorities: Week [X] (Q[Y])

**Top 3–5 Weekly Priorities**:
1. [GTM] Outbound: Send 150 targeted emails to Call Center Ops Directors in UK.
2. [Product] Ship Gusto Direct Webhook API v1 and deploy to production.
3. [Product] Fix 2 reported CSV export edge cases for night-shift hours.
4. [Growth] Publish SEO Comparison Article: "AttendFlow vs Manual Spreadsheets".
5. [Ops] Complete Bus Factor audit for customer support onboarding script.

**Active Weekly Big Bet / Experiment (`#os_experiment` or `#growth_experiment`)**:
- *Hypothesis*: "Adding a 60-second video demo to the landing page hero section will increase sandbox preview clicks by 15%."
- *Metric Target*: Sandbox click-through rate ≥ 8.5%.
```

---

## 🔍 3. Friday Retrospective & Review Prompts

At 4:00 PM every Friday, leadership and domain owners answer 4 review prompts:

1. **OKR Alignment Check**: *"Did this week's completed priorities directly advance our top 2 quarterly OKRs in `YEAR_1_QUARTERLY.md`?"*
2. **Experiment Outcome**: *"What did we learn from this week's active experiment, and how does it adjust next week's bet?"*
3. **Focus & Health Check**: *"Did anyone exceed working hour caps or violate anti-focus rules (`FOCUS.md`, `FOUNDER_3_5_YEAR.md`)?"*
4. **Friction Identification**: *"What process or documentation friction did we encounter that requires an update in AttendFlow OS?"*
