## 🎯 1. Founder Personal Goals & Non-Negotiables

### Personal Financial & Life Outcomes
- **Financial Autonomy**: Build a sustainable, cash-generative SaaS asset producing significant enterprise value while maintaining optionality for founder liquidity or long-term dividends (`FINANCE_LIFESTYLE_3_5_YEAR.md`).
- **Autonomy & Mastery**: Retain strategic control over company vision and culture while mastering high-leverage CEO leadership skills.

### Non-Negotiable Health & Lifestyle Boundaries

## 🔄 2. Founder Role Evolution (2026–2031)

```text
[ Phase 1: The Builder (Months 1–12) ]
Focus: 80% Product & Direct Sales Execution
• Writing code, running sales demos, handling initial customer success calls, 
  and configuring GTM outbound sequences.

            │
            ▼
[ Phase 2: The Operator & Manager (Months 12–36) ]
Focus: 50% GTM & Product Strategy / 50% Team Building
• Recruiting key domain leads (SDRs, Senior Engineers), formalizing playbooks, 
  delegating routine ops, and managing company capital (`CAPITAL.md`).

            │
            ▼
[ Phase 3: The Architect & CEO (Months 36–60) ]
Focus: 80% Capital Allocation, Executive Leadership & Strategic Partnerships
• Driving multi-year vision, high-stakes partnerships, M&A/Capital conversations 
  (`ACQ_CONVERSATIONS.md`), and preserving company culture at scale.
```

## 📊 3. Founder Energy & Sustainability Scorecard

Tracked monthly during the founder's personal review block:

- **Energy Rating (1 – 10)**: Target ≥ 8.0/10 average monthly energy level.
- **Clarity & Calm Rating (1 – 10)**: Target ≥ 8.5/10 subjective sense of strategic direction and freedom from fire-fighting chaos.
- **High-Leverage Time Allocation**: Target ≥ 70% of weekly time spent on Tier 1 & Tier 2 priorities (`PLAYBOOK_FOUNDER.md`).
