- [ ] Read `ATTENDFLOW_OS.md`, `VISION.md`, and `CULTURE.md`.

## 🔒 3. Team Member Offboarding Protocol

When a team member or contractor exits the organization, complete these 4 steps within **2 hours**:

1. **Access Revocation**: Deprovision Google Workspace, GitHub, Slack, HubSpot, and AWS/Cloud hosting accounts.
2. **IP & Asset Handover**: Ensure all local code repositories, design files, and documentation updates are pushed to central repositories.
3. **Exit Interview**: Conduct a 30-minute exit conversation to capture feedback on company processes and culture.
4. **Legal & Non-Disclosure Audit**: Confirm compliance with Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (PIIA).
