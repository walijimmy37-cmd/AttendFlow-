This document establishes financial targets, founder compensation models, personal wealth goals, and strategic trade-offs over a 3–5 year horizon.

---

## 💵 2. Founder Compensation & Dividend Policy

- **Phase 1 (Months 1–12)**: Survival salary baseline ($60,000 - $90,000 / year) to minimize company cash burn.
- **Phase 2 (ARR > $1M)**: Market-rate CEO salary ($150,000 - $220,000 / year) + annual performance bonus tied to ARR growth & NRR targets.
- **Phase 3 (ARR > $3M & Profitable)**: Quarterly founder dividend distributions (up to 30% of net profits) while retaining healthy cash reserves (`CAPITAL.md`).

*Primary Choice: AttendFlow defaults to the Capital-Efficient Path, reserving Venture Hypergrowth strictly if the 4 filters in `FUNDING_DECISIONS.md` are activated.*
