## 📍 2. Role-Based Navigation Entry Points

Find your role below to locate the primary documents powering your work:

### 👑 For Founders & Executive Leadership
1. Start with `HOW_TO_USE_ATTENDFLOW.md` for a high-level overview.
2. Review long-term vision in `VISION.md` and capitalization in `CAPITAL.md`.
3. Reference `PLAYBOOK_FOUNDER.md` for weekly time-allocation and board routines.
4. Monitor weekly metrics via `DASHBOARDS.md`.

### 📣 For Growth, Marketing & Sales (GTM Team)
1. Read `PLAYBOOK_GTM.md` for your complete 30-day onboarding and tactical index.
2. Study ICP definitions in `GTM.md` and conversion math in `FUNNEL.md`.
3. Master cold sequences in `OUTBOUND.md` and content schedules in `INBOUND.md`.
4. Review sales scripts and objection handling in `SALES_ENABLEMENT.md`.

### 🛠️ For Product Engineering & Customer Success
1. Read `PLAYBOOK_PRODUCT_CS.md` for roadmap priorities and support escalation rules.
2. Review feature roadmap in `ROADMAP.md` and prioritization rules in `DECISION_FRAMEWORKS.md`.
3. Track user sentiment and feedback logs in `CUSTOMER_LEARNING.md`.

### ⚙️ For Operations, Legal & Finance
1. Read `PLAYBOOK_OPS.md` for monthly financial close and compliance checklists.
2. Reference revenue models in `REVENUE.md` and financial guardrails in `CAPITAL.md`.
3. Check legal compliance requirements in `LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md` and risk triggers in `RISKS.md`.
