# AttendFlow - 3–5 Year Strategic North-Star Narrative

This document defines the 3–5 year strategic narrative, current baseline, 5-year destination targets, and the multi-year story arc for AttendFlow.

---

## 📍 1. Where We Are Today (Honest Current State)

- **Product & Infrastructure**: Production-ready full-stack application featuring 3-second 1-click check-ins, real-time floor availability maps, geofence/IP verification, and audit-ready CSV exports formatted for Gusto, Rippling, and Quickbooks.
- **GTM & Traction**: Validated early messaging in target ICPs (software houses, digital agencies, call centers). Operational cold outreach sequences, content calendar, and 14-day interactive sandbox preview (`https://attendflow.io/#preview`).
- **Team & Capacity**: Lean founder-led execution supported by specialized contractors (UI/UX design, full-stack dev).
- **Current Fragilities**: Early stage distribution repeatability; high dependence on founder bandwidth for live sales demos and customer onboarding.
