# AttendFlow - Strategic Focus & Anti-Focus ("What We Won't Do") Spec

This document details the 3–5 year strategic themes, priority hierarchy, and strict "What We Won't Do" anti-focus boundaries for AttendFlow.

---

## 🎯 1. Three Core Strategic Themes (2026–2031)

1. **Win Workforce Operations for SME Tech Agencies & Call Centers**: Become the indisputable #1 attendance and floor availability platform for software houses, BPO call centers, and multi-location agencies before expanding into legacy verticals.
2. **Eliminate Payroll Administration Friction**: Achieve < 60-second audit-ready payroll export workflows across Gusto, Rippling, Quickbooks, and regional payroll providers.
3. **Build an Anti-Fragile, High-Margin Business**: Maintain high gross margins (> 85%), high Net Revenue Retention (> 115%), and lean operational density over headcount expansion.

---

## 🏆 2. Priority Hierarchy

When resource or strategic trade-offs arise, team members evaluate options using this non-negotiable hierarchy:

```text
1. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) & Customer Delight 
   └── OVER ──> Raw Top-of-Funnel Logo Growth

2. Deep Feature Mastery in Core ICPs (Tech / BPO / Call Centers)
   └── OVER ──> Shallow Feature Breadth Across 10 Different Industries

3. Sustainable Capital-Efficient Profitability
   └── OVER ──> Unchecked "Growth-At-All-Costs" Burn

4. Product-Led Automated Onboarding & Speed (< 50ms UI response)
   └── OVER ──> Bespoke Professional Services & Custom Consultations
```
