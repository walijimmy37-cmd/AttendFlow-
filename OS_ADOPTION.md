# AttendFlow - OS Adoption & Launch Strategy Plan

This document outlines the internal launch plan, 30-60-90 day adoption milestones, and enablement tactics to embed the AttendFlow OS into daily operations.

---

## 🚀 1. Internal Launch Strategy (The OS Walkthrough)

Treat the rollout of AttendFlow OS as an internal product launch:

1. **30-Minute Launch Session**: Conduct a mandatory walkthrough with all core team members and contractors.
2. **Core Narrative**: Demonstrate how `HOW_TO_USE_ATTENDFLOW.md` and `ATTENDFLOW_OS.md` reduce daily friction, eliminate repetitive decision fatigue, and clarify strategic priorities.
3. **Live Demonstration**: Walk through a simulated weekly alignment using `OPERATING_RHYTHM.md` and `DASHBOARDS.md`.

## 💡 3. Adoption Reinforcement Tactics

- **Workspace Pinning**: Pin `HOW_TO_USE_ATTENDFLOW.md` and `ATTENDFLOW_OS.md` to the top of internal Slack/Teams channels and Notion/Workspace dashboards.
- **Meeting Directives**: In all operational meetings, reference docs explicitly (e.g., *"Let's check `DECISION_FRAMEWORKS.md` before approving this feature bet"*).
- **Public Wins**: Celebrate wins during Friday retrospectives where OS playbooks directly saved time or improved conversion rates.
