This document details the testimonial acquisition framework, 1-page case study format, trust badge roadmap, and customer survey templates for AttendFlow.

```text
"Before AttendFlow, we [Problem / Conflict]. After deploying AttendFlow, we [Action Taken], resulting in [Quantifiable Metric: e.g. 70% reduction in admin / 14% boost in billable hours]. It's the cleanest workforce tool we've used."
```
