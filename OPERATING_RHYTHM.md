# AttendFlow - Weekly Operating Cadence & Execution Rhythms

This document outlines the weekly meeting structures, functional time-blocking routines, and review rituals that drive consistent execution for AttendFlow.

---

## 📅 1. Weekly Operating Cadence Overview

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Monday Morning (30m)  | Tue - Thu Execution   | Friday Afternoon (45m)|
| Kickoff & Alignment   | Time-Blocked Blocks   | Review & Retrospective|
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| - Review top metrics  | - Outbound prospecting| - Metric review vs target|
| - Top 3 wins / blockers| - Content creation   | - Log A/B experiment  |
| - Commit 1-3 weekly bets| - Product UX iterations| - Update 90-day plan |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
```

---

## ⏰ 2. Weekly Leadership / Ops Meeting Template (30–45 Mins)

### Agenda (Mondays @ 09:00 AM)
1. **Metric Review (10 mins)**:
   - Unique Visitors, Qualified Leads, Demos Held, New Paid Accounts, MRR, Churn.
2. **Wins & Blockers (15 mins)**:
   - What went well last week?
   - What friction point is slowing down trial-to-paid conversions?
3. **Weekly Focus & Experiments (15 mins)**:
   - Select 1 to 3 targeted bets for the week (e.g., A/B copy tweak, partner outreach sprint, new blog post).

---

## 🧩 3. Time-Blocking Allocation Guide for Operators

To maintain focus, structure the working week into protected functional blocks:

- **Outbound Sales & Prospecting**: 3 x 60-minute blocks (Tue, Wed, Thu mornings).
- **Content Creation & SEO**: 2 x 90-minute blocks (Mon afternoon, Wed afternoon).
- **Product & UX Polish**: 2 x 60-minute blocks (Tue afternoon, Thu afternoon).
- **Customer Success & Demo Calls**: On-demand / scheduled via Calendly slots (Mon-Fri 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM).

---

## 🔁 4. Friday Retrospective & Review Ritual (45 Mins)

Every Friday at 4:30 PM, complete the following 4-step checklist:

- [ ] Log weekly telemetry numbers into `DASHBOARDS.md` or central metric sheet.
- [ ] Record results of active experiments in `EXPERIMENTS.md` (Keep, Kill, or Iterate).
- [ ] Synthesize qualitative user feedback from the week into `CUSTOMER_LEARNING.md`.
- [ ] Review sprint progress against the active 30-day milestone in `90_DAY_PLAN.md`.
