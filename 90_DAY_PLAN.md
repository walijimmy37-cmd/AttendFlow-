# AttendFlow - 90-Day Execution Plan

This document establishes the tactical 90-day roadmap for AttendFlow, divided into three 30-day execution sprints focused on acquisition, activation, and channel expansion.

---

## 🏃 90-Day Sprint Roadmap Overview

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Sprint 1: Days 1–30   | Sprint 2: Days 31–60  | Sprint 3: Days 61–90  |
| Foundations & Outbound| Conversion & Pilots   | Channel & Scaling     |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| - Launch ICP outbound | - Close first 10 paid | - Launch partner prog |
| - Publish 4 blogs     |   design partners     | - Expand inbound SEO  |
| - Target: 5k visitors | - 1-Page Case Studies | - Target: $8.5k MRR   |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
```

---

## 🟢 Sprint 1: Days 1–30 (Foundations, Outbound & Initial Traction)

### Focus & Objectives
Establish outbound engine, test messaging across ICPs, publish initial content assets, and generate first 15 qualified sales leads.

### Key Activity Targets
- [ ] Build target prospect database of 500 decision-makers (Operations Directors in ICP 1 & 2).
- [ ] Launch 6-touch email & LinkedIn outbound sequence to all 500 contacts.
- [ ] Publish 4 flagship blog posts on `attendflow.io` targeting high-intent attendance terms.
- [ ] Release Lead Magnet #1 (*Workforce Attendance & Grace Period Policy Template*).

### Key Outcome Targets
- **Traffic**: 3,000 Unique Visitors.
- **Leads**: 90 Qualified Demo Requests.
- **Demos Held**: 30 Live Demos.
- **New Paid Customers**: 5 Paid Accounts (**$550 MRR**).

---

## 🟡 Sprint 2: Days 31–60 (Conversion Optimization & Proof Points)

### Focus & Objectives
Optimize landing page conversion funnel based on A/B test results, convert trial users into paid customers, and publish first 2 case studies.

### Key Activity Targets
- [ ] Run A/B Headline & CTA Experiment (`?hero=v1` vs `?hero=v2`).
- [ ] Conduct 1-on-1 feedback calls with all early active trial users.
- [ ] Produce and publish 2 detailed 1-page Customer Case Studies (1 Agency, 1 BPO).
- [ ] Activate automated email nurture campaign for demo drop-offs.

### Key Outcome Targets
- **Traffic**: 5,000 Unique Visitors.
- **Leads**: 150 Qualified Demo Requests.
- **Demos Held**: 60 Live Demos.
- **New Paid Customers**: 15 Paid Accounts (**$2,550 MRR total**).

---

## 🔵 Sprint 3: Days 61–90 (Channel Partnerships & Scaling)

### Focus & Objectives
Formally launch AttendFlow Partner Program, onboard first 5 HR consultant partners, and expand inbound content distribution.

### Key Activity Targets
- [ ] Outreach to 50 HR consultants and fractional CHROs with partner proposal.
- [ ] Sign and onboard 5 active referral/implementation partners.
- [ ] Publish 4 additional vertical-specific blog posts (Healthcare, Call Centers, Tech).
- [ ] Launch retargeting social campaigns targeting sandbox visitors.

### Key Outcome Targets
- **Traffic**: 8,000 Unique Visitors.
- **Leads**: 240 Qualified Demo Requests.
- **Demos Held**: 95 Live Demos.
- **New Paid Customers**: 35 Paid Accounts (**$6,500+ MRR total**).
