# AttendFlow - The Founder & Company Operating Contract (2026–2031)

This contract serves as the ultimate compass document for AttendFlow—synthesizing our 3–5 year narrative, strategic non-negotiables, working agreements, and review rhythms.

---

## 📜 1. The Core Purpose & 5-Year Vision

AttendFlow exists to **eliminate workforce operations chaos and administrative friction for growing businesses globally**. 

By 2031, we will serve **2,000+ companies and 100,000+ employees daily**, generating **$10M–$25M ARR with > 30% net profit margin**, while maintaining a high-trust, high-density team of under 45 people.

---

## 🎯 2. Strategic Focus & Non-Negotiables

We commit to building a focused, anti-fragile business. We will **NEVER**:
1. Build bespoke one-off code for single enterprise accounts.
2. Manufacture or sell physical hardware/biometric devices.
3. Chase feature bloat outside core workforce, shift, and payroll operations.
4. Raise venture capital without satisfying unit economics and growth filters.
5. Sacrifice team health or founder wellbeing for short-term vanity metrics.

---

## 🤝 3. How We Work (Operating Principles & Culture)

- **Obsess Over Time Saved**: Every UI interaction, API call, and internal meeting must optimize for speed and simplicity.
- **Evidence Over Opinion**: Data, customer telemetry, and A/B test results override titles or assumptions.
- **Written-First Autonomy**: We communicate asynchronously, document decisions in AttendFlow OS, and grant execution autonomy to domain owners.
- **Radical Clarity & High Standards**: We give direct, kind feedback and measure outcomes against clear benchmarks (`METRICS_INDEX.md`).
