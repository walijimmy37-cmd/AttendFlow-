## 💎 2. Core M&A Valuation Drivers

Acquirers evaluate workforce management SaaS targets based on 4 critical value drivers:

1. **Net Revenue Retention (NRR ≥ 115%)**: Demonstrates sticky account usage and expansion beyond initial team size.
2. **Deep Integration Moat**: Native, zero-friction connectivity with Gusto, Rippling, Quickbooks, Slack, and MS Teams.
3. **ICP Vertical Dominance**: Uncontested leadership within high-margin niches (e.g. software houses, call centers, multi-site healthcare).
4. **Capital Efficiency (Rule of 40 ≥ 50%)**: Combining high revenue growth rate (%) with high free cash flow margin (%).
