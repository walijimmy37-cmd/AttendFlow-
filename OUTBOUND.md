# AttendFlow - Outbound Sales Playbook

This document details target list building criteria, persona-specific messaging frameworks, and multi-touch outbound sequences for AttendFlow.

---

## 🎯 1. Target Account List Building Criteria

- **Target Geography**: United States, United Kingdom, Pakistan, UAE / GCC.
- **Company Size**: 15 to 300 employees.
- **Target Roles**: Operations Director, VP of Operations, HR Manager, Founder & Managing Director.
- **Data Sources**: LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Crunchbase, Apollo.io, Clutch.co.
