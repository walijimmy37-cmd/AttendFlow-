# AttendFlow - Year 1 Quarterly OKR Plan (Q1–Q4)

This document specifies the quarterly themes, objectives, key results, and OKR targets for Year 1, laddering up to `3_5_YEAR_NARRATIVE.md` and `YEAR_1_STRATEGY.md`.

---

## 🗓️ Q1: "Prove the Core Loop" (Months 1–3)

**Quarterly Theme**: Validate value proposition, onboard initial 25 paying accounts, and perfect Gusto/Rippling CSV exports.

- **Objective 1: Achieve Initial Product-Market Fit in Core ICPs**
  - **KR1**: Reach $3,500 MRR across 25+ active paying accounts in software houses and call centers.
  - **KR2**: Achieve 14-day trial-to-paid conversion rate ≥ 20.0%.
  - **KR3**: Maintain 30-day cohort retention ≥ 85.0% and gross monthly logo churn < 2.5%.
- **Objective 2: Build First Predictable Outbound & Inbound Funnel**
  - **KR1**: Send 1,200 targeted outbound cold emails with open rate ≥ 45% and reply rate ≥ 3.5%.
  - **KR2**: Generate 45 qualified live product demos via `https://attendflow.io/#preview`.
  - **KR3**: Publish 8 SEO-optimized articles targeting "Gusto attendance integration" and "Call center floor map software".
- **Objective 3: Establish Operational Discipline & Security Baseline**
  - **KR1**: 100% adherence to Monday alignment and Friday retro cadence (`OPERATING_RHYTHM.md`).
  - **KR2**: Complete initial SOC 2 Type 1 readiness self-audit (`LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`).

## 🗓️ Q2: "Make Growth Repeatable" (Months 4–6)

**Quarterly Theme**: Scale outbound sequences, launch direct payroll webhooks, and hire First SDR.

- **Objective 1: Scale Revenue to $8,000 MRR**
  - **KR1**: Expand active paying client base to 60+ accounts ($8,000 MRR milestone).
  - **KR2**: Increase Net Revenue Retention (NRR) to ≥ 110% via account seat expansion.
  - **KR3**: Reduce CAC Payback Period to < 4.5 months.
- **Objective 2: Automate Integration & Workflow Moat**
  - **KR1**: Release 1-click direct API integration for Gusto and Rippling.
  - **KR2**: Launch Tablet Kiosk Mode for BPO call center reception desks.
  - **KR3**: Maintain platform UI latency < 50ms across all check-in endpoints.
- **Objective 3: Expand Team Capacity & Onboarding**
  - **KR1**: Recruited and successfully onboarded Hire #1 (Dedicated SDR) using `ONBOARDING.md`.
  - **KR2**: SDR reaches 15 qualified demos booked per month within 30 days of hire.

## 🗓️ Q3: "Deepen Retention & Expansion" (Months 7–9)

**Quarterly Theme**: Optimize cohort retention, launch shift roster management, and unlock partner channels.

- **Objective 1: Scale Revenue to $14,000 MRR**
  - **KR1**: Reach $14,000 MRR across 100+ active accounts.
  - **KR2**: Lower gross monthly logo churn to < 1.8%.
  - **KR3**: Achieve 20% of new monthly leads through partner referrals (`PARTNERSHIPS.md`).
- **Objective 2: Expand Product Value & Absence Risk Insights**
  - **KR1**: Release Shift Roster & Overtime Manager module.
  - **KR2**: Launch AI-driven Absence Risk Pattern Alerts.
  - **KR3**: Achieve feature adoption rate ≥ 40% across active tenant admins within 30 days.
- **Objective 3: Onboard Customer Success Lead**
  - **KR1**: Onboard Hire #2 (CS & Onboarding Specialist) to handle user onboarding calls and tickets.
  - **KR2**: Maintain average support ticket resolution time < 2 hours.

## 🗓️ Q4: "Prepare to Scale" (Months 10–12)

**Quarterly Theme**: Achieve $20,000+ MRR, establish cash-flow profitability, and finalize Year 2 expansion roadmap.

- **Objective 1: Reach Year 1 North-Star Financial Target**
  - **KR1**: Reach $20,000+ MRR ($240,000+ ARR) across 150+ active client accounts.
  - **KR2**: Achieve cash-flow profitability with net profit margin ≥ 15%.
  - **KR3**: Maintain Net Revenue Retention (NRR) ≥ 115%.
- **Objective 2: Harden Platform for Multi-Region Expansion**
  - **KR1**: Complete SOC 2 Type 2 compliance audit certification.
  - **KR2**: Deploy multi-time-zone shift rotation support for UK/GCC clients.
  - **KR3**: Maintain 99.95% application uptime across Cloud Run infrastructure.
- **Objective 3: Complete Annual OS Compass Review**
  - **KR1**: Conduct December Annual Compass Review (`ATTENDFLOW_CONTRACT.md`).
  - **KR2**: Publish Year 2 Strategy & OKR spec.
