# AttendFlow - Content & Messaging Architecture

This document defines the 3-level message hierarchy, core content pillars, and modular content blocks used across landing pages, ad campaigns, email sequences, and sales collateral.

## 🏗️ 1. Message Hierarchy

### Level 1: Core Brand Promise
> **"Workforce operations, simplified. 1-Click attendance, automated leave tracking, and 60-second payroll exports."**

### Level 2: Core Value Pillars
1. **Frictionless Tracking**: 1-Click web & mobile check-in under 3 seconds with IP and geofence verification.
2. **Operational Visibility**: Live daily roster maps, department filters, and automated tardiness exception logs.
3. **Payroll Efficiency**: Audit-ready 1-click exports compatible with Rippling, Gusto, Deel, and Quickbooks.
4. **Enterprise Control**: Role-based permissions, custom grace period rules, and 256-bit encrypted audit logs.

### Level 3: Supporting Proof Points
- **98.4%** Average customer punctuality improvement.
- **70%** Reduction in monthly HR administrative workload.
- **60-Second** Payroll export execution time.
- **WCAG AA & 100 A11y** Accessibility rating.
