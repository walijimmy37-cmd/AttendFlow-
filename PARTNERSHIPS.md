# AttendFlow - Channel & Partnerships Playbook

This document defines the strategic partner ecosystem, program tier structure, partner outreach sequences, and co-marketing bundle options for AttendFlow.

---

## 🤝 1. Strategic Partner Segments

1. **HR Consultants & Fractional CHROs**: Recommend workforce management tools to growing clients.
2. **Payroll Service Bureaus & Bookkeepers**: Seek automated, error-free attendance CSV feeds for client payroll runs.
3. **IT Managed Service Providers (MSPs)**: Bundle cloud software solutions with core IT infrastructure management.
4. **Regional Business Associations**: Software house associations, call center federations, and retail trade groups.
