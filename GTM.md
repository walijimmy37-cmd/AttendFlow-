# AttendFlow - Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy & Target Segments

This document defines the primary Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs), GTM strategic motions, and core offer structures for AttendFlow.

---

## 🎯 1. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

AttendFlow targets 3 primary ICP segments based on operational complexity and team size:

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| ICP 1: Hybrid Tech    | ICP 2: Call Centers   | ICP 3: Multi-Site     |
| Agencies & Studios    | & BPOs                | Retail & Healthcare   |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| - Size: 15–150 staff  | - Size: 50–500 seats  | - Size: 5–30 sites    |
| - Tech: Jira, Slack   | - Tech: CRM, Avaya    | - Tech: Mobile, POS   |
| - Pain: Unbilled hrs  | - Pain: Tardiness     | - Pain: Site presence |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
```

### ICP 1: Hybrid Software & Tech Agencies (15–150 Employees)
- **Profile**: Software consultancies, digital agencies, design studios.
- **Key Stakeholder**: Agency Owner, VP of Operations, Resource Manager.
- **Tech Stack**: Slack, Jira, Google Workspace, Rippling/Gusto.
- **Primary Pain Point**: Lost revenue from unbilled client hours and lack of visibility into developer core-hour availability.
- **Offer Match**: Growth Plan ($49/mo base + $3/user) with client project tagging and 1-click Gusto/Rippling CSV exports.

### ICP 2: Call Centers & BPO Operations (50–500 Seats)
- **Profile**: Customer support centers, telemarketing, back-office operations.
- **Key Stakeholder**: Director of Operations, Call Center Floor Manager.
- **Tech Stack**: Zendesk, Five9, Excel, QuickBooks.
- **Primary Pain Point**: Excessive tardiness, shift turnover, complex multi-shift rotation, and end-of-month attendance disputes.
- **Offer Match**: Growth or Enterprise Plan with live floor availability maps, automated tardiness grace periods, and SMS shift alerts.

### ICP 3: Multi-Site Retail & Healthcare Chains (5–30 Locations)
- **Profile**: Urgent care clinics, dental groups, retail stores, fitness chains.
- **Key Stakeholder**: Operations Director, Regional HR Manager.
- **Tech Stack**: POS software, WhatsApp groups, paper sign-in sheets.
- **Primary Pain Point**: Inability to verify physical on-site presence, buddy punching, and regulatory overtime compliance risks.
- **Offer Match**: Enterprise Plan with geofenced mobile check-in, tablet kiosk mode, and 256-bit encrypted audit trails.

## 🚀 2. GTM Strategic Motions (6–12 Month Horizon)

1. **Inbound & Content Marketing**: Educational content targeting high-intent search terms (e.g., "attendance tracking for agencies", "BPO late grace period policy").
2. **Outbound Cold Outreach**: Targeted LinkedIn + email multi-touch sequences directed at Operations Managers & HR Directors in ICP 1 & 2.
3. **Channel & Partner Ecosystem**: Co-marketing and referral agreements with fractional CHROs, HR consultants, and payroll bureaus.
4. **Product-Led Growth (PLG) Sandbox**: Free 14-day interactive demo preview (`attendflow.io/#preview`) driving self-serve trial signups and instant sales lead qualification.
