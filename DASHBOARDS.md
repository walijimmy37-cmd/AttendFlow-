# AttendFlow - Operational Dashboards & Scorecards

This document defines the 4 core scorecards used by leadership, marketing, sales, and product engineering to monitor AttendFlow's health.

---

## 👑 1. Founder & Executive Scorecard (Weekly)

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            FOUNDER / CEO WEEKLY SCORECARD                       |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Metric                             | Current Value         | Weekly Target      |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)    | $2,550                | + $400 / wk        |
| Total Active Paid Accounts         | 24 Accounts           | + 3 Accounts / wk  |
| Gross Logo Churn Rate              | 1.8% / mo             | < 2.0% / mo        |
| Net Revenue Retention (NRR)        | 108%                  | ≥ 110%             |
| Cash Runway / Burn                 | 18 Months             | ≥ 12 Months        |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
```

---

## 📣 2. Growth & Marketing Scorecard (Weekly)

- **Total Unique Visitors**: Target ≥ 1,250 / week.
- **Visitor-to-Lead Rate**: Target ≥ 3.0% (Hero CTA + Demo Modal).
- **Inbound Qualified Leads**: Target ≥ 35 Leads / week.
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**: Target ≤ $25 / lead.
- **Top Channel Attribution**: Tracking % of leads from SEO, Outbound, Direct, and Social.

---

## 💼 3. Sales & Pipeline Scorecard (Weekly)

- **Demos Scheduled / Held**: Target ≥ 12 Held / week.
- **Lead-to-Demo Conversion Rate**: Target ≥ 40.0%.
- **Demo-to-Paid Conversion Rate**: Target ≥ 25.0%.
- **Average Deal Size (ARPA)**: Target ≥ $120 / month.
- **Sales Cycle Length**: Target ≤ 10 Days from demo to paid account.

---

## ⚙️ 4. Product & Customer Success Scorecard (Weekly)

- **Trial Activation Rate**: Target ≥ 65% (Users who test at least 2 report tabs or export CSV).
- **1-Click Export Execution Frequency**: Total CSV/XML exports generated per active account.
- **On-Page Feedback Sentiment**: Target ≥ 85% "Positive" rating on footer widget.
- **Support Ticket Resolution Time**: Target ≤ 2 Hours for first response.
