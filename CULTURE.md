## 🛠️ 2. Operating Principles & Behavioral Norms

- **Written-First Communication**: We prioritize clear, structured markdown documents (`GTM.md`, `OPERATING_RHYTHM.md`, `ROADMAP.md`) over unstructured meetings.
- **Asynchronous Respect**: We respect deep work blocks. Instant messaging is for urgent blockers; strategic discussion happens in documented proposals.
- **Radical Transparency**: Weekly metrics, customer feedback, churn reasons, and financial runway are shared openly across the team.
- **Extreme Ownership**: If a team member spots friction in user onboarding or sales pipeline, they own proposing the solution.
