## 🛡️ 3. Non-Negotiable Operational Guardrails for Year 1

Aligned with `FOCUS.md` and `FOUNDER_3_5_YEAR.md`:

1. **Zero Bespoke Code**: Refuse custom feature builds for single accounts that do not serve at least 20% of our ICP base.
2. **Strict Time Boundaries**: Founder working hours capped at **50 hours/week** with mandatory offline Sundays.
3. **Runway Floor**: Maintain a strict **18-month minimum cash reserve** (`CAPITAL.md`, `RESILIENCE_DESIGN.md`).
4. **Circuit-Breaker Enforcement**: Automatically pause feature expansion if cohort 30-day retention drops below 50% (`STRESS_TESTS.md`).
