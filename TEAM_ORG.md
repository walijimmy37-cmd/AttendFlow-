## 📋 2. Core Role Profiles & KPI Benchmarks

### Role 1: Sales Development Representative (SDR)
- **Primary Focus**: Outbound list building, cold email/LinkedIn outreach, and demo booking.
- **Reporting Line**: Reports to Founder / CEO.
- **Key KPIs**:
  - Qualified Demos Held: ≥ 15 / month.
  - Outbound Reply Rate: ≥ 3.5%.

### Role 2: Customer Success & Onboarding Lead
- **Primary Focus**: Trial activation, payroll CSV integration onboarding, and account retention.
- **Reporting Line**: Reports to Founder / CEO.
- **Key KPIs**:
  - Trial Activation Rate: ≥ 70%.
  - Gross Monthly Logo Churn: < 1.5%.

### Role 3: Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Node/React)
- **Primary Focus**: Core product engineering, HRIS integration webhooks, and security compliance.
- **Reporting Line**: Reports to Technical Founder / CTO.
- **Key KPIs**:
  - Production Uptime: ≥ 99.9%.
  - Feature Delivery Velocity: 100% of sprint commitments.
