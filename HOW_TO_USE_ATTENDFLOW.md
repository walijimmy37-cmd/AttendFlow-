## 🌟 1. What AttendFlow Is Building

AttendFlow is a **modern workforce operations and attendance management platform** designed for growing businesses (software houses, call centers, multi-site healthcare/retail chains). 

We help operations leaders and founders eliminate attendance administration chaos by combining:
- **3-Second 1-Click Clock-ins** (Web & Mobile with IP & Geofence verification)
- **Real-Time Floor Availability Maps** (With automated tardiness grace periods)
- **60-Second Audit-Ready CSV Payroll Exports** (Formatted directly for Gusto, Rippling, and Quickbooks)

## 🧭 2. How We Make Decisions

At AttendFlow, we follow 4 core operating principles (`CULTURE.md`):

1. **Obsess Over Time Saved**: Software value comes from speed and administrative reduction.
2. **Default to Action**: Execute small, high-impact experiments rather than debating opinions.
3. **Evidence Over Opinion**: Data from user telemetry and A/B tests always overrides titles or assumptions.
4. **Craft & Frictionless Simplicity**: Keep UI fast, clean, and intuitive under 50ms response times.

## ⚡ 5. How We Think About Uncertainty, Crises & Big Decisions

We design AttendFlow to be anti-fragile, thriving amidst market changes:

- **Scenario Futures & Early Warning**: We model 4 strategic scenarios (`SCENARIOS.md`) backed by quantitative signal triggers (`TRIGGERS.md`).
- **Funnel & Channel Stress-Tests**: We pre-model financial shocks and enforce product circuit breakers (`STRESS_TESTS.md`).
- **Crisis Response**: We execute standardized emergency protocols within 24–72 hours (`CRISIS_PLAYBOOKS.md`).
- **Capital & Acquisition Frameworks**: We evaluate funding raises (`FUNDING_DECISIONS.md`) and M&A proposals (`ACQ_CONVERSATIONS.md`) against strict quantitative filters.
- **Safety Margin Design**: We embed financial (18-mo runway floor), operational (Bus factor cross-training), and cognitive (max 3 concurrent bets) margins into our architecture (`RESILIENCE_DESIGN.md`).
