## 📋 4. Weekly RevOps Review Rhythm

Every Monday morning, evaluate these 5 metrics:

1. **New Inbound Leads**: Target ≥ 35 leads/week.
2. **Demos Held**: Target ≥ 12 demos/week.
3. **Trial-to-Paid Conversions**: Target ≥ 3 new accounts/week.
4. **Net New MRR Added**: Target ≥ $400/week.
5. **Logo Churn Count**: Target ≤ 1 account/week.
