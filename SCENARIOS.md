# AttendFlow - Scenario Planning & Strategic Futures Architecture

This document defines the 4 core macro scenarios, key market uncertainties, early warning indicators, and actionable playbooks for AttendFlow.

---

## 🎲 1. Key Business Uncertainties

AttendFlow operates in a dynamic B2B SaaS environment governed by 6 core uncertainties:

1. **Market Willingness-to-Pay**: Do SMEs prioritize automated attendance tracking vs basic free spreadsheets?
2. **Competitive Landscape**: Will major HRIS platforms (Gusto, Rippling, Workday) build native low-cost attendance modules?
3. **Macro Economy**: How will SME budget tightening and hiring freezes impact seat expansion?
4. **Product Activation Rate**: Can self-serve trial users achieve 1-click CSV export value without customer success intervention?
5. **Outbound Channel Fatigue**: Will cold email deliverability and LinkedIn outreach response rates remain effective?
6. **Key Talent Retention**: Can AttendFlow recruit and retain top-tier engineering and sales talent in a competitive market?

## 📖 3. Detailed Scenario Specifications & Playbooks

### Scenario A: Base Case ("Capital-Efficient Engine")
- **What's True**: Steady execution of 90-day sprints (`90_DAY_PLAN.md`). Outbound reply rate ≥ 3.5%, trial activation ≥ 65%, logo churn < 2.0%/month.
- **Strategic Priority**: Maintain disciplined execution, optimize conversion funnel, and achieve cash-flow profitability at $20k MRR.
- **First 30-Day Action Plan**:
  1. Continue weekly operating cadence (`OPERATING_RHYTHM.md`).
  2. Optimize Gusto & Rippling CSV export templates.
  3. Expand content publishing to 2 blog posts per week.

---

### Scenario B: Upside ("Venture-Scale Hypergrowth")
- **What's True**: Inbound demo requests exceed 60/week. Payback period drops under 3 months. Inbound investor interest from tier-1 seed funds.
- **Strategic Priority**: Responsibly compress hiring timelines (`TEAM_ORG.md`), scale paid acquisition, and evaluate strategic Seed financing ($1.5M - $3.0M).
- **First 30-Day Action Plan**:
  1. Activate Hire #1 (Dedicated SDR) and Hire #2 (Full-Stack Engineer) immediately.
  2. Increase monthly paid experiment budget to $3,000 with a strict $25 CPL ceiling.
  3. Prepare investor data room and pitch deck (`CAPITAL.md`).
- **Guardrails**: Maintain a minimum 18-month cash runway and do not inflate permanent fixed burn above $25k/month until MRR exceeds $35k.

---

### Scenario C: Downside ("Macro Squeeze & High Churn")
- **What's True**: Visitor conversion drops below 1.5%, logo churn spikes above 3.5%, and CAC payback stretches beyond 7 months.
- **Strategic Priority**: Cut non-essential spend, freeze non-revenue hiring, and narrow ICP focus strictly to ICP 1 (Tech Agencies with high billing leakage).
- **First 30-Day Action Plan**:
  1. Freeze contractor spend and pause unproven ad channels.
  2. Conduct 1-on-1 exit interviews with all cancelled accounts to identify churn root cause (`CUSTOMER_LEARNING.md`).
  3. Test packaged annual prepay discounts (30% off) to secure upfront cash reserves.

---

### Scenario D: Disruption ("Platform Competitive Shock")
- **What's True**: Gusto or Rippling launches a free native attendance tracker, commoditizing basic check-in functions.
- **Strategic Priority**: Pivot positioning away from basic check-ins toward specialized enterprise capabilities (geofenced audit logs, tablet kiosk mode, custom IP whitelisting).
- **First 30-Day Action Plan**:
  1. Update landing page copy (`POSITIONING.md`) to emphasize security, geofencing, and complex BPO shift rotation features that basic HRIS platforms lack.
  2. Accelerate partnership outreach to regional IT MSPs and HR Consultants (`PARTNERSHIPS.md`).
  3. Launch custom API webhook integration for enterprise clients.
