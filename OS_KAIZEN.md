## 🧪 2. Small-Bet OS Experiments (`os_experiment`)

To test process optimizations, we run short 2-week experiments logged in `EXPERIMENTS.md` with tag `#os_experiment`:

- **Example Experiment A**: *"Shorten `PLAYBOOK_GTM.md` by 30% and test if new SDR onboarding time drops from 5 days to 3 days."*
- **Example Experiment B**: *"Replace 15-minute sync with a structured 3-bullet markdown update in `#leads-attendflow`."*

---

## ✂️ 3. The Sunset & Simplification Rule

Any document or process specification in the AttendFlow OS that has not been actively cited, reviewed, or updated in **2 consecutive quarters** is immediately flagged for review:

- **Action**: Either rewrite the document with a clear operational purpose OR archive it to `/archive/` per `OS_VERSIONING.md`.
