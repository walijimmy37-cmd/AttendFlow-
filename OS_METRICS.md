# AttendFlow - OS Effectiveness Metrics & Health Index

This document defines the quantitative and qualitative health metrics used to evaluate whether AttendFlow OS is accelerating execution.

---

## 📐 1. OS Effectiveness Metrics Dashboard

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            ATTENDFLOW OS HEALTH DASHBOARD                       |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Health Metric                      | Target Benchmark      | Review Cadence     |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Time-to-Onboard New Hire           | ≤ 3 Days to 1st Win   | Per Onboarding     |
| Time-to-Strategic Decision         | ≤ 48 Hours            | Monthly            |
| Rate of Revisited Decisions        | < 5% of Total Decs    | Quarterly          |
| Quarterly Team Clarity Score       | ≥ 4.5 / 5.0 Rating    | Quarterly          |
| Unprocessed OS Feedback Backlog    | < 3 Open Items        | Monthly            |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
```

---

## 🔍 2. Diagnostic Review Questions

During quarterly reviews, leadership evaluates OS health using 3 diagnostic questions:

1. *"Is the OS making us faster and clearer, or slower and more bureaucratic?"*
2. *"Are team members actively quoting playbooks in meetings, or defaulting to un-documented assumptions?"*
3. *"Which specific playbook or framework introduced friction over the last 90 days?"*
