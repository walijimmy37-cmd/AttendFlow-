## 📞 2. Live Demo Execution & Discovery Framework

During sales demos, follow the 5-step framework outlined in `SALES_ENABLEMENT.md`:

1. **Discovery & Qualification (2 Mins)**: Ask: *"How are you currently tracking team attendance and shift rotations?"* & *"How long does month-end payroll CSV preparation take?"*
2. **Employee Clock-in Experience (1 Min)**: Demonstrate 3-second 1-click check-in with IP/geofence verification.
3. **Manager Presence Heatmap (2 Mins)**: Show real-time floor availability, department filters, and automated tardiness grace periods.
4. **1-Click Payroll Export (1 Min)**: Trigger real-time audit-ready CSV export formatted for Gusto/Rippling.
5. **Closing & Trial Setup (1 Min)**: Launch pre-configured 14-day demo workspace (`attendflow.io/#preview`).
