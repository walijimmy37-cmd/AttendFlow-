# AttendFlow - Inflection Point Framework: Capital & Funding Decisions

This document establishes the evaluation criteria, funding option trade-offs, and decision filters for evaluating external financing.

---

## 🚦 1. Capital Raise Evaluation Filters

Before pursuing external capital, leadership evaluates 4 non-negotiable prerequisites:

```text
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            CAPITAL RAISE DECISION FILTERS                        |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| Prerequisite Filter                | Minimum Benchmark Threshold                 |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| 1. Clear Product-Market Fit        | Net Revenue Retention (NRR) ≥ 110%          |
| 2. Unit Economics Scale            | LTV : CAC Ratio ≥ 6.0x                      |
| 3. Payback Velocity                | CAC Payback Period < 5.0 Months             |
| 4. Channel Repeatability           | > 30 Qualified Inbound Demos / Week         |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
```
