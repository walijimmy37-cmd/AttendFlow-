## ⚡ 3. Personal Operating System & Focus Rules

1. **Protect Deep Work**: Block 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM on Tuesdays and Thursdays for uninterrupted coding, content writing, or strategy work.
2. **Standardized Demo Slots**: Schedule all sales demos into designated blocks (Mon-Fri 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM) via Calendly link.
3. **Daily Shutdown Ritual**: Spend 10 minutes at 5:30 PM writing down the top 3 priorities for the following day.
